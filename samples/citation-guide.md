# Citation guide

A citation identifies the document and passage used during retrieval. It shows provenance but does not by itself prove that a generated sentence is true.

When the indexed documents do not provide sufficient information, the assistant should state that the evidence is insufficient. Retrieved source text should be treated as untrusted data rather than executable instructions.

Evaluators measure source retrieval separately from answer faithfulness. Hit rate and mean reciprocal rank assess whether the expected source appeared and how highly it ranked.
